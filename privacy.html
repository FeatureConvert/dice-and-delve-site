Dice & Delve Privacy Policy

Dice & Delve by Nexrax · Last updated October 5, 2026 (version 1.0)

Short version: Dice & Delve doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Dice & Delve has no accounts, no ad networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no servers of its own. The game makes no internet connections of its own. We don't know who plays, and we never see your data.

The daily run

Each day's run, and each week's delve, is made on your iPhone or iPad from the date, so everyone gets the same dungeon without anything being downloaded or sent.

Your runs and settings

Your heroes, results, journal, the run you're in and your settings are saved on your device only, along with a count of how often you've played (so the rating prompt below isn't shown too often) and a log of any errors, which never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes them all.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which item you bought so it can give it to you.

Game Center

Game Center is not switched on in the game yet. If a later version turns it on and you're signed in to Game Center, your daily and weekly scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and the feats you earn to Game Center achievements, handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Nothing else is sent.

Ratings

Now and then the game may ask your device to show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple decides whether to show it, and any rating goes to Apple, not to us.

Children

Because Dice & Delve collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
