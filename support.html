Common questions

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap Settings on the title screen, then Restore Purchases. (The Heroes screen has a Restore button too.) The Full Game and any dice sets come back on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

How does the daily run work?

Everyone gets the same dungeon, the same skeletons and the same dice each day. A new one starts at 09:00 UTC. Your first run of the day sets your score; later tries are practice and don't change it.

I closed the app in the middle of a run. Is it lost?

No. The run waits for you exactly where you left it. Open the game and carry on.

Can I see the first room's lesson again?

Yes. Tap Settings on the title screen, then Play the first room again.

Are there ads?

No. Never. No timers or energy either: play as much as you like.
