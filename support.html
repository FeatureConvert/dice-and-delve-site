Common questions

I bought something but don't have it.

Tap Settings on the title screen, then Restore Purchases. (The Heroes screen has a Restore button too.) The Full Game and any dice sets come back on any iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account.

How does the daily run work?

Everyone gets the same dungeon, the same skeletons and the same dice each day. A new one starts at 09:00 UTC. Your first run of the day sets your score; later tries are practice and don't change it.

How does the weekly delve work?

Tap Weekly on the title screen. Everyone gets the same dungeon and dice for the week, with a twist that changes each week. A new one starts on Mondays at 09:00 UTC. Your first try of the week sets your score; later tries are practice.

What is Heroic?

Win a dungeon and a Heroic tick box appears beside it on the title screen. Tick it for the same rooms with tougher skeletons and less rest. A Heroic win puts a gold star on your hero in the Journal and earns a feat.

The game moves too much for me.

Tap Settings on the title screen and switch on Reduce motion: the dice land without tumbling, nothing springs or pulses, and the door doesn't swing.

I closed the app in the middle of a run. Is it lost?

No. The run waits for you exactly where you left it. Open the game and carry on.

Can I see the first room's lesson again?

Yes. Tap Settings on the title screen, then Play the first room again.

Are there ads?

No. Never. No timers or energy either: play as much as you like.
