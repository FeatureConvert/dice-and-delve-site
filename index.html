Dice & Delve

A pocket dungeon crawl that plays like a board game. No ads, ever.

Support · Privacy policy

Roll three dice, put them on Move, Strike and Guard, and outwit skeletons who show you what they'll do next. Every turn is a small puzzle.

Free to play: the first dungeon, the Knight and the Rogue, a daily run and a weekly delve that are the same for everyone, and a Journal of your heroes' records, feats and every skeleton you've met. One purchase, the Full Game, opens three more heroes, two more dungeons with their own skeletons and bosses, and endless mode. Win a dungeon and its Heroic version opens, for when you want it harder. Dice sets change the look of your dice and nothing else.

For iPhone and iPad, in English, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese. It plays offline.
