Dice & Delve

A pocket dungeon crawl that plays like a board game. No ads, ever.

Support · Privacy policy

Roll three dice, put them on Move, Strike and Guard, and outwit skeletons who show you what they'll do next. Every turn is a small puzzle.

Free to play: the first dungeon, the Knight and the Rogue, and a daily run that's the same for everyone. One purchase, the Full Game, opens more heroes and endless mode. Dice sets change the look of your dice and nothing else.
